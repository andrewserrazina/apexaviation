HUTTO & TAYLOR
Planning a Taylor Municipal Airport (T74) Location

Private Pilot Flight Training Near Hutto and Taylor, TX

Apex Aviation is exploring a Part 61 flight-training operation at Taylor Municipal Airport (T74) with a planned Piper PA-28 under lease negotiation — a potential option for prospective students in Hutto and Taylor. Airport approval, parking, and aircraft arrangements are still pending, and March 2027 is our current planning target, not a confirmed date.

Where Would I Train?

Our preferred location is Taylor Municipal Airport (T74), a public, non-towered airport (CTAF 122.8) owned by the City of Taylor, sitting about two nautical miles west of downtown Taylor. It's a short, direct drive from both Hutto and Taylor — close enough that recurring weekday and weekend lessons are realistic without a long commute eating into your training time.

We haven't published an exact drive time for this route, since the final lesson location (ramp space, parking, where you'd meet your instructor) isn't settled yet. If you want to see the drive for yourself in the meantime, here's a direct link: get directions to Taylor Municipal Airport (T74) →

Important: T74 is not yet an operating Apex Aviation location. We're actively working through airport approval, ramp/parking arrangements, and aircraft logistics before we can confirm it. Everything on this page describing in-person training at T74 is a proposal we're planning toward, not a service available today.

When Can I Start?

March 2027 is our planning target for opening the proposed Taylor Municipal Airport location — it is not a confirmed launch date. Airport approval, parking, and aircraft arrangements are all still pending, and the timeline could move depending on how those pieces come together. If you join the waitlist below, we'll keep you updated as each piece is confirmed, well before any public enrollment opens.

What Would It Cost?

The rates we're proposing for this location are $185/hour for the aircraft, with fuel included, and $75/hour for instruction — $260 total for an hour that bills both. These are proposed rates for planning purposes, not a locked-in price list: ground instruction, checkride fees, and other training costs are separate and will affect what your total training actually costs.

We're not publishing a guaranteed number of hours to finish your certificate or a bundled package price. Every student progresses at a different pace, and a specific hour-count promise or discounted package isn't something we can responsibly commit to before we even know our own costs at a confirmed location. For the broader picture of what Apex charges across all of its programs, see our full pricing page.

Never Flown Before? Only Done a Discovery Flight?

Most students start with the basics: how the aircraft controls work, straight-and-level flight, climbs and descents, and getting comfortable simply being in the airplane. If you've only taken a single discovery flight, that's a completely normal starting point — those first few lessons build the foundation everything else sits on, regardless of where you end up training.

You don't have to wait for the Taylor location to start preparing. Apex Advantage Private Instruction is live right now — 1-on-1, online, $55/hour, every certificate level — and it's a practical way to start building your aeronautical knowledge, work through the private pilot written test material, and get comfortable with the concepts before you ever need to book flight time. Can I Fit Training Around My Schedule?

Flight training works best on a recurring schedule — the same day and time slot most weeks — rather than booked one lesson at a time whenever something opens up. Recurring windows protect your spot, keep skills from going stale between lessons, and make it much easier for your instructor to plan a curriculum that actually builds week over week instead of re-covering the same ground. When you join the waitlist below, tell us two times that would work for you most weeks — it helps us understand what a realistic schedule looks like for Hutto and Taylor-area students before we finalize how we structure lesson availability.
