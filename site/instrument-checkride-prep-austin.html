Who This Is For

Instrument rating applicants in the final weeks before their practical test -- whether you're training with Apex or finishing up elsewhere. If your flight portion is done or nearly done and you want structured, honest prep for the oral, this is built for that stage specifically.

What the Instrument Oral Actually Tests

Your DPE grades against the Instrument Rating Airman Certification Standards -- not a trivia list. Sessions are built around the real areas of operation:

Preflight Planning & Procedures

IFR Clearances & ATC Procedures

Flight by Reference to Instruments

Navigation Systems

Instrument Approach Procedures

Emergency Operations

How Prep Sessions Work

Each session is 1-on-1 with Andrew, booked by the hour through Apex Advantage Private Instruction. There's no fixed script -- sessions typically work through:

  • Mock-oral-style questioning across the ACS areas above
  • Scenario-based discussion -- weather decisions, system failures, and the judgment behind them, not just rote answers
  • Honest identification of where your knowledge is actually thin, so you spend prep time where it matters
  • Regulations and systems review specific to the aircraft and route you'll fly your checkride in

How This Is Different

vs. Checkride Prep System

The Checkride Prep System is a self-paced question bank and AI practice tool, live for Private Pilot today. This page is live, instructor-led instrument prep -- a different format, while instrument content there is in development.

vs. Instrument Rating Training

Instrument Rating Training is the full ground-to-checkride program. This page is narrower on purpose -- just the final-stretch oral and checkride prep, for applicants who are already most of the way there.
