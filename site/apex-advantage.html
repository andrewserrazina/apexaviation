How Ground School Works

Attend the classes
you need.

Apex Advantage Ground School is modular by design. Students can join an individual class for $25, attend only the topics they want, join at any point in the schedule, and return for future topics later — or enroll in the complete Ground School program for $400.

No Cohort

No cohort to fall behind in and no requirement to start with the first class.

Join Anytime

Classes repeat on the schedule, so you can join the topic you need whenever it comes back around.

Your Pace

Pay $25 per class as you go, or lock in the complete 20-class program for $400 whenever you're ready.
