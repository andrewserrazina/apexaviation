Serving Pilots from Liberty Hill and the Surrounding Area

Liberty Hill has been one of the fastest-growing communities in Central Texas, drawing families and professionals who bring a passion for aviation with them. Despite its rapid growth, Liberty Hill pilots have had limited access to structured, scenario-based flight training without a long commute into Austin proper. Apex Aviation is changing that — our single IFR-equipped aircraft is accessible from Liberty Hill in under 35 minutes and offers the kind of professional, curriculum-driven instruction previously reserved for students at major Part 141 schools.

What Training with Apex Looks Like

Every lesson follows the same structure, whether it's your first hour or your fortieth.

1

Brief

Set a goal for the lesson and talk through the decisions you'll practice.

2

Fly

Practice the maneuver or scenario with one-on-one coaching.

3

Debrief

Review what went well, what needs work, and what to prepare for next time.
