The Experience

This isn't another
study session.

You'll be evaluated through realistic oral questioning, similar in style to what you may experience during your practical test — not quizzed from flashcards.

DPE-Style Follow-Ups

Your instructor probes your answers the way an examiner does — asking "why?" and "what would you do?" instead of moving on after the first correct answer.

Scenario-Based Decision Making

Real go/no-go and in-flight scenarios, not just fact recall — the kind of judgment questions that show up in a real oral.

Aircraft-Specific Questions

Tailored to the aircraft and avionics you actually fly, based on what you tell us during intake.

Full ACS Coverage

Structured to touch the knowledge and risk-management areas the ACS expects you to know.

Risk Management

Aeronautical decision-making questions that probe judgment, not just procedure.

Personalized Feedback

A written Performance Report afterward — what you know, where you struggle, and what to study next.

We can't know exactly what your specific DPE will ask — every examiner is different. This mock oral is built to build real understanding and judgment, which is what actually transfers to checkride day.
