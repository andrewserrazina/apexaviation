Serving Pilots from Georgetown and Williamson County

Georgetown has become one of the fastest-growing cities in the United States, drawing retirees, remote workers, and young families to Williamson County's rolling terrain north of Austin. Georgetown Municipal Airport (KGTU) serves as the local GA hub — a busy single-runway field with instrument approaches and a growing ramp population. For Georgetown pilots wanting structured, scenario-based flight training, the drive south into Austin brings access to Apex Aviation's single IFR-equipped Cessna 172, where training is built around real-world decision making rather than checkbox completion.
