Who We Are

Modern aviation education, built beyond the minimum standard.

Apex Aviation Training Group LLC is the company behind Apex Advantage, a training platform for private, instrument, and commercial pilot candidates. The mission is straightforward: provide professional, checkride-focused aviation education that goes beyond minimum FAA standards and prepares students to become safer, more competent pilots.

Today, Apex serves pilots through individual online ground sessions, mock oral exams, and self-paced digital preparation. Training is tailored to each pilot's knowledge gaps and goals, with an emphasis on practical scenarios and sound decision-making.

Led by a Commercial Pilot and CFI/CFII, Apex is developing a Part 61 flight school in the Austin area. Aircraft, airport arrangements, and financing are being evaluated before an opening date is announced.

Personal instruction. Practical preparation.

Leadership

Built and operated by a CFI/CFII.

Andrew Serrazina
Commercial Pilot · CFI/CFII · Founder

Andrew Serrazina is a Commercial Pilot and Certified Flight Instructor with an instrument instructor rating (CFI/CFII) who founded Apex Aviation Training Group to build the kind of checkride and flight training resources he wished had existed as a student: structured, scenario-based, and focused on genuine understanding rather than memorization.

His work with students informs Apex's individual ground instruction, mock oral exams, and digital preparation resources. He is leading the company's planned expansion into Austin-area flight training and aircraft partnerships.
